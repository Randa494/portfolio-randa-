Real-Time Driver Monitoring System Using YOLO & MediaPipe

Graduation project — an AI-powered driver safety system that uses computer vision and deep learning to detect dangerous driving behaviors as they happen, before they become accidents.

Problem: Distracted, drowsy, and unbuckled driving remain leading causes of road accidents, with no affordable real-time safeguard for everyday vehicles.

Solution: A live video-analysis pipeline combining YOLOv8 object detection with MediaPipe FaceMesh landmark tracking to flag risk factors and trigger instant alerts.

  • Seatbelt detection
  • Mobile phone usage detection
